#F4: What percentage of games each season did Olympiacos attempt more free throws than their opponents?

  ?olympiacosStatline bball:freeThrowsAttempted ?olympiacosFTA .
  ?opponentStatline bball:freeThrowsAttempted ?opponentFTA .
